The journey, as rhythm — sixteen frames at a fifth of full size

Six acts, seven crossings, two held beats and one action beat, in the order a visitor meets them. The seven full frames are the real compositions, scaled; the crossings are sketches of the moment between two statements, and the word in each is the horizon.* fragment the content layer already carries for the chapter it leads into. Read this sheet for four things and nothing else: whether the density rises and falls or merely rises, whether the Altimeter appears rarely enough to matter, whether the type progression is a system or a ramp, and whether the background moves without announcing itself.
