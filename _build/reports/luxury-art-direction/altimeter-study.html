The altimeter — current presentation against a restrained one

One asset, photographed twice. Nothing about the model, its materials or MeridianLights.tsx is modified — both plates are renders of the shipped stratos-altimeter.glb, in the same pose, at the same dial size, on the same near-black field. What changes is what a photographer changes: how much ambient light fills the shadows, how long the lens is, how much of the separation is done by the rim rather than by the key, and what exposure the highlights are held at. The question §49 asks is whether the instrument's tech-object character is a property of the object or of how it has been lit. It is mostly the second.

Current · ambient 0.50 · fill 1.7 · 28° lens · exposure 1.06 · grayscale(1), opacity .94

The four lamps at their shipped values. There is no true black anywhere on the object: the ambient fills the housing's interior and the shadow side with an even blue, so the form is read from tone rather than from light, which is the render signature of a game asset. The 28° lens over an object this small bows the bezel. It is then desaturated to grey and dimmed to 94% to stop it competing — two corrections applied to a presentation problem rather than to its cause.

Restrained · ambient 0.14 · fill 0.70 · 17° lens · exposure 0.95 · rim-led · no desaturation, no fade

The same object with the ambient brought from 0.50 to 0.14, the fill from 1.7 to 0.70, the lens lengthened from 28° to 17° at a matched distance and the exposure lowered to 0.95, and the separation handed from a cold key to a neutral rim. The housing now has a true black side and a drawn edge. It needs neither the grayscale filter nor the opacity: at full strength it reads as a machined object that has been lit, which is what §14 asks for and what the fade was standing in for.

One change at a time — which of the four does the work

A · current. The shipped recipe, in the shipped in-frame treatment.

B · deep blacks. Ambient 0.50 → 0.14, fill 1.7 → 0.70. Nothing else. This is the largest single step in the study.

C · a longer lens. 28° → 17° at a matched distance, exposure 1.06 → 0.95. The bezel stops bowing.

D · rim-led. Key reduced and neutralised, rim raised. The silhouette separates from a near-black field without raising exposure.
