Why body text uses clay 700, not clay 500

Clay 500 on ivory 300 measures about 2.9:1 — it fails WCAG AA for text at every size. It is a surface colour. Clay 700 clears 4.5:1 and is the accent you set text in.

Long-form prose

Long-form switches to the serif at 18px with 1.8 leading and a hard cap of 68 characters. The measure is the point: past about 75 characters the eye loses the line return, and the reader starts skimming without deciding to.

Sans is for interface. Serif is for reading. Mixing them the other way round is the fastest way to make a product feel like a template.
