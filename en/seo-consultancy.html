The work

What do you get
every month?

Foundation

Technical SEO

Loading speed, mobile layout, indexing, heading structure, structured data, internal links. Visitors don't see this layer, but this is the layer Google sees.

Local

Google Business Profile and local search

For a local business the map result often brings in more than the website itself. We set it up, categorise it, fill it in and keep it in order every month.

AI search

Appearing in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini recommendations

More and more people ask an AI search tool when they are looking for a provider. We shape your site and the information about it so that these recommendations can find you too.

Research

Keywords and intent

We look for the question your customer actually types, not the biggest search volume. A narrower phrase that brings a buyer is worth more than a broad one that brings a browser.

Pages

What's missing or doesn't answer

If the discovery shows that an important service of yours has no page of its own, or the existing one doesn't answer what people ask, we write or rewrite it, the way you would explain it yourself. Exactly which pages is itemised in the proposal.

Report

Monthly summary

Rankings, traffic, next steps. Every month you get a readable summary of what moved, what didn't, and what we are doing next month.

The price

What does SEO
actually cost?

We work on a monthly fee, because SEO is ongoing work. We name the fee after the discovery, because until then we wouldn't know what we were promising.

What drives it

The state of the site

On a fresh site with a clean structure, the technical layer is sorted quickly. On one that has been patched up for years, the same work takes much longer, and sometimes rebuilding is less work than repairing. The discovery tells us which, not a hunch.

What drives it

The field you compete in

A landscaper in a small town has to work for a few dozen phrases; a national online shop for several thousand. That is why there is no list price: the fee is the amount of work it takes to come up in your field.

How long it takes

We think in months

The effect usually starts to show within 3–6 months. That is why we don't plan for a single month: the measurements show what is worth continuing and what isn't.

What you know in advance: after the discovery you get a specific, itemised proposal for what happens in the first months, and there is no line in it we can't justify.

If the discovery shows that, for you, ads or a better website would bring more from the same budget right now, we tell you so, and we don't sell you SEO.

The model is a monthly fee because in SEO a one-off intervention goes stale. A technical fix lasts until somebody touches the site; a page that has been written keeps working for as long as the question it answers stays relevant.

If your site needs rebuilding too, a search-optimised website is built as a one-off project, with SEO already in its first version.

Where we work

Local and national
SEO

We work across the whole country, online, and half our clients are in Budapest. The work is the same everywhere; local search isn't.

Where the map decides

For a provider outside the capital, the search is usually “service + town” or “nearby”, and a good share of the results sit in the map box. Here the Google Business Profile, the service area and the reviews count for at least as much as the website itself. At Rapidkert, too, this was the layer that moved first.

Where the field decides

In Budapest and on national phrases, an order of magnitude more businesses compete for the same search, and the map decides less often. Here content and the technical layer carry most of the work: the winner is whoever's page answers what was typed more precisely.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us — we're glad to help.

How long before results show?

Technical fixes can show within a few weeks, because Google re-crawls the site. Real movement usually starts to show within 3–6 months. Anyone promising faster is either selling advertising under the name of SEO, or using a method that will cost you later.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and nobody else can either. Rankings are decided by Google's algorithm, which changes constantly. What we can commit to is the work: what we measure, what we fix, what we write, and a readable report on it every month.

What does SEO cost?

It depends on the state of the site and the size of the field you compete in. A local service business needs considerably less work than a national online shop. After the discovery you get a concrete proposal for what we will do in the first months.

Do I need a new website for it?

Not necessarily. Most existing sites have enough room to make starting worthwhile. If discovery shows that the site's structure or speed would waste most of the work, we say so, and fix the website first instead.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

Advertising brings traffic immediately and stops when the budget runs out. SEO starts more slowly, but a ranking you have earned keeps working when you are not spending. The two complement each other: at Rapidkert they ran in parallel, and each brought in quote requests on its own.

What does the monthly fee include?

A monthly report, technical SEO, management of your Google Business Profile and local search, visibility in AI search tools, and writing or rewriting the pages identified in the discovery. The exact scope is itemised in the proposal.

Do you write regular blog posts?

Not as part of the standard monthly fee. If the discovery shows that content would bring you the most, we set it out separately in the proposal.

Can I appear in ChatGPT's recommendations?

We work on that too: AI search tools draw on your website, your Business Profile and the public information about you. We put those in order and make them consistent. Appearing there cannot be guaranteed either.

Do I need an SEO company or an independent consultant?

A consultant is enough when you need an answer to one specific question and someone to carry out the advice. If you don't have that person, a company is the better choice, where the same team measures, writes and fixes the site.

If I stop, do I lose everything?

No. What we wrote and fixed stays on your site and keeps working. Rankings can slip over time if nobody looks after them, but they don't vanish overnight, and we switch nothing off.
