The difference

A logo is a mark.
An identity
is a system.

If all you have is a logo, your business card, your website and your posts easily look like three different companies. In a brand identity the logo, colours, typefaces and usage rules work together, the same way on every surface.

Good work is the baseline, but on its own it doesn't show. Your brand identity shows that you are good before anyone has tried you. With a well-thought-out identity you appear on the same level as the big players, and you come across as larger and more trustworthy than your size.

A brand identity works when it can be repeated. So alongside the images we also hand over rules: which colour goes where, which typeface at what size, and what never changes.

Brand guidelines come with it, setting out the correct logo versions, the exact colour codes, the typefaces and the rules of the image style. So anyone who works for you (printers, ad managers, social media managers) can use your brand consistently.

Help

Frequently asked
questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us — we're glad to help.

What does a brand identity include? Do I just get a logo?

Much more: a logo, a colour palette, typefaces, an image style and the tone you communicate in, all set out in brand guidelines.

What is the difference between a logo and a brand identity?

A logo is a mark. A brand identity is the set of rules that makes every surface of yours look like the same company.

I already have an identity. Do we redesign it or build on it?

It depends how strong the existing base is. If your logo and colours work well, it is often enough to build on them and turn them into a system. If they are inconsistent or dated, a redesign pays off more in the long run. The first step is always a thorough review.

How do you price it?

With a one-off, itemised project fee that we give you in advance, after the free discovery call. If you need a website too, the two can be made in one project.

How long does it take, and what do I need to provide?

We set the deadline in the proposal and guarantee it, provided your feedback and materials arrive on time. All we need from you is the chance to get to know your business: who your clients are and what sets you apart.

Do I get brand guidelines?

Yes. They contain the logo versions, the colour codes, the typefaces and the rules of the image style.

Why does this matter for a small company?

Because customers see how you look before they see your work. With a consistent identity you come across as larger and more trustworthy than your size.

Do I get a say?

Throughout. We check in with you at the key points, and three rounds of revisions are included.

Will you align it with the website and the ads?

If you want, yes: the same team makes the identity, the website and the ads, so everything is consistent from the ad to the moment someone gets in touch.

Can I use the files freely?

Yes. The finished identity is yours, in every format you need: in print, on social media, in ads or on your website.
