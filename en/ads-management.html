01The signal path

From the ad to the enquiry there are six stations.

  1. 01IntentSomeone is searching for something, or is in a situation your service answers.
  2. 02MessageThe ad answers that situation, and doesn't praise you in general terms.
  3. 03ClickIt is only worth anything if the person who arrives is the one it was for.
  4. 04Landing pageThe page has to continue what the ad started.
  5. 05EnquiryThe form or the phone call. Commercially, this is the number that counts.
  6. 06FeedbackWhat the measurement shows is where the next round starts.

The situation

Sound familiar?

You started a campaign. Google takes the budget every day, and at the end of the month you get a screenshot of the clicks. How many phone calls and quote requests came of it, nobody can tell you.

Or now and then you press the Boost button on your Facebook page and hope it shows the post to the right people.

We start with measurement: first we set things up so you can see what brings in enquiries, and only then do we spend more on what works.

Two channels

for you, or must

Google Ads

Google: when they are already searching

Anyone who types in “plumber Kecskemét” wants to buy. Here you have to be there at the moment they decide.

  • Keyword research and a structure built around search intent
  • Bidding strategy and budget allocation
  • Ad copy and extensions
  • Google Business Profile and map listings
  • For online shops: Google Shopping and Performance Max, with a product feed
Meta Ads

Facebook and Instagram: when they aren't searching yet

Here you approach people in their feed, with a good offer and a striking image or video. First you have to win their attention; interest comes after that.

  • Audience research and segmentation
  • Creative: image, video, ad copy
  • Retargeting and lookalike audiences based on your existing customers
  • Setting up the Meta pixel and measuring enquiries

The process

How ads management
actually runs

Advertising is the only channel where the first enquiries can arrive on the day you switch it on. Meaningful results, though, take time.

Free discovery call and proposal

We talk through where your clients are, which channel suits you and what budget it makes sense to start with.

Research and setup

Audience and competitor analysis, sharpening the offer, and building the campaigns in your own Google and Meta accounts. Setting up measurement, so we see numbers from day one.

Learning phase, usually 2–4 weeks

The first weeks are about gathering data. We launch several messages, targeting options and creatives, and the data decides which direction works.

Improving and expanding

Usually after 4–6 weeks a stable direction emerges: which ad, audience and message bring in the most enquiries. From then on we put the budget on the combinations that work and switch off the rest.

The price

What does ads
management cost?

There are two items, and they are not the same thing: the ad budget, which goes to Google and Meta, and the management fee, which comes to us. You set the budget.

The budget

What the platform gets

This money goes to Google and Meta, and you can see it to the last forint in your own account. The typical market range for small businesses is a starting budget of HUF 80,000–150,000 a month: below that it is hard to gather enough data to improve on. We tailor the exact budget to your market and your goals.

The fee

What the management gets

A monthly fee for the work, kept separate from the budget: audience research, ad copy, creatives, bidding strategy, continuous improvement and measurement. The platform is only a tool: the return depends on how the budget is spent. That is the work, and that is what the management fee pays for.

One fee, every platform

Google and Meta together

Managing the Google and Meta campaigns is covered by one fee, because the two channels compete for the same budget. That way someone is always looking at their combined result.

The accounts remain yours. We build the campaigns in your account and get manager access. If you moved to someone else, nothing would need rebuilding: the history and the learning stay in the account.

No one can honestly promise a specific number of clients or a revenue figure. What we commit to: a professional setup, improvement based on measurement, a transparent monthly report, and telling you when something isn't working.

If our conversation shows that SEO would get more out of your budget right now, we'll tell you. Advertising is fast, but it only delivers while you pay for it; the two work best together.

Measurement

We measure what brings in money.

What people usually ask about advertising

You don't need to. From you: a few photos of your work, and a few minutes a month for the report.

Help

Frequently asked
questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us — we're glad to help.

What ad budget should I start with, and how does that relate to the management fee?

These are two separate items: the ad budget, which goes to Google or Meta, and the management fee, which we receive for the work. The typical market range for small businesses is a starting budget of HUF 80,000–150,000 a month, because below that it is hard to gather enough data. We always tailor the exact budget to your market and your goals.

When will I see the first results?

Advertising is the only channel where the first enquiries can arrive on the day you switch it on. Meaningful results, though, take time: the first 2–4 weeks are usually a learning and data-gathering phase, and a stable direction usually emerges after 4–6 weeks.

Are Google or Meta ads better value for my business?

It depends on whether your clients search for you or you have to find them. Anyone who types in “plumber Kecskemét” wants to buy: that is Google. In the feed, you approach them: that is Facebook and Instagram. For an online shop, Google Shopping and Performance Max come into play too. For many businesses, the two together bring the best results.

How do I know the advertising pays off?

Instead of likes and impressions, we look at the business result. We set up tracking of how many quote requests, calls, form submissions or purchases came from the ads, and what they cost. You get this in a monthly report, in a clear format.

What happens to the ad account if I cancel?

The ad account remains yours; that is a principle for us. We always build the campaigns in your own Google and Meta accounts, to which we get manager access. If we part ways, the account, the data and the campaigns we built stay with you.

Why do I pay a monthly management fee if Google and Meta get the ad spend anyway?

Because the platform is only a tool: how your money is spent decides whether it pays off. A badly set-up campaign spends your budget just the same, only without results. For the management fee we do the work: audience research, ad copy, creatives, bidding strategy, continuous improvement and measurement.

Do I need a finished website, or is a Facebook page enough?

You need a place the ad leads to, where the person can make a decision. A landing page written for this, or a well-built website, is best, because there we can measure too. If you don't have one, with us the website and the advertising are in one pair of hands.

Do you guarantee more clients?

No one can honestly promise a specific number of clients or a revenue figure; if someone does, be careful. What we commit to: a professional setup, continuous improvement based on measurement, a transparent report, and telling you when something isn't working.

Who writes the ad copy and makes the creative?

Writing the copy and building the campaigns is our job; it is part of the service. For images, it is best if we get material of your actual work or products, because a real picture often performs better than a stock photo. A good phone shot of your finished work is often worth more than any staged set.

What is the difference between advertising and SEO?

Advertising brings traffic immediately, and stops when you turn off the tap. SEO builds up more slowly; its effect usually starts to show within 3–6 months, but after that it brings enquiries without ad spend. The two complement each other.

Do you run LinkedIn or TikTok ads?

No. We focus on three platforms: Google (Search, Shopping, Performance Max), Facebook and Instagram. That is where we can deliver the most.
