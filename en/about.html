4Years of continuous research

If you have worked with an agency before, you may know this: at the first meeting the boss sits across from you, after that you write to an account handler, someone else builds the website and a third company runs the ads. When something doesn’t work, everyone points at someone else. That is why we work differently.

01Why we stay small

You talk to the people who do the work.

We work as if we were part of your team: we put ourselves in your position, we say so when we think something isn’t worth it, and we look for the best direction together. For you, it means you only have to say once what you want, and the people you told carry it through.

Direct contact
No go-between between the decision and the work: you deal with the people who design and build your site.
One pair of hands
Design, development, copy, SEO and advertising stay inside one project, so every question and every fault has one owner.
Craft
Every interface is built in custom code, without a template, to a level of detail we would happily put in our own portfolio.
Discipline
Measurable goals, a documented handover, and the source code and access are yours.

Help

Frequently asked
questions

Can’t find your answer? Write to us.

Who are you?

Stratos Webdesign is a Hungarian web design agency. We build websites in custom code, 3D websites and product configurators, online shops and brand identities, and carry them forward with monthly search engine optimisation and Google and Meta ads management. The partners: Artúr Lukács (founder) and Richárd Tímár (ads management).

Where do you work?

Across Hungary. The work is quicker online: design, feedback and handover all happen on video calls. If the project calls for it, we come to you in person, wherever you are. We work in Hungarian, English and German.

How does working together start?

With a free discovery call. After it we send you a tailored proposal in writing, valid for 14 days. If you say yes, the work starts once the deposit arrives: design, development, handover.

How do you price your work?

The website is a one-off project fee, and it is yours. You can add SEO and maintenance alongside it as a monthly partnership. We don’t publish a price list, because every proposal is individual: we give you the fee after the free discovery call, in writing and itemised.

How long does a website take?

It differs from project to project. We set the exact deadline in the proposal and guarantee it, provided everything we need from you arrives on time.

What do you guarantee?

Two things: the deadline, provided everything we need from you (text, images, access) arrives on time, and three rounds of revisions during design.

How bespoke will my website be?

Entirely. We build it in custom code, without templates or page builders, tailored to your brand, your customers and your goals.

Can it be extended later?

Yes. New pages, features or languages can be added later, and because the code is yours, you are not tied to us.

What happens after handover?

You get the access and the files. If you like, with monthly maintenance we look after hosting, updates and backups, and with monthly SEO we keep taking the site forward.

Do you manage ads as well?

Yes. We build and run Google Ads, Google Shopping and Meta (Facebook, Instagram) campaigns, with measurement, so you can see what brought in enquiries.

How do I see what the website brings in?

We set up measurement (for example Google Analytics and Meta Pixel), so you can see how many people come, from where, and how many enquiries they turned into. With SEO and advertising you also get a monthly report.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No, nobody can honestly guarantee that. The effect of SEO usually starts to show within 3–6 months, and every month you see where we stand.
