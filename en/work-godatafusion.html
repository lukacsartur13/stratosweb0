GoDataFusion — construction surveying

Reality into data. Shown on one building.

GoDataFusion does three things: it records a site with a 360° camera, surveys the terrain, and calculates quantities from drawings. The site does not list them — it walks the visitor through them on a single 3D building.

Sector construction, surveying What we delivered website, 3D web experience, SEO Languages Hungarian, English, German Live at godatafusion.com

01Client context

Three services the buyer had never seen.

360° site documentation, site surveys and quantity takeoff from drawings are not self-explanatory services to a contractor or developer. Until they can see what they get at the end, it looks like one more cost line.

So the job was not to describe the three services but to show how one and the same site becomes an image, a terrain model and a spreadsheet.

023D web experience

One building, three readings

At the centre of the page stands a 3D model of a three-storey building. As you scroll, the same model transforms: as the visitor moves down, each service reveals a different layer of it.

Capture — point cloud and capture points
In the 360° camera chapter the building is drawn as a point cloud, surrounded by the capture points. The visitor sees what the service hands over at the end: a digital state you can walk through.
Measure — terrain, with contour lines
For the site survey the model becomes a wireframe, and beneath it the terrain's contour drawing appears, with the calculated area and earthwork volume.
Quantify — floor plan, with counts
For quantity takeoff the building flattens into the floor plan of a single level, and the values counted from the drawing appear around it: floor area, doors, windows, rooms.
An interface tuned to each service
On the opening screen, hovering over any service switches the whole interface. Each chapter has its own colour, which follows the model and the figures too.
Honest sample data
Every figure says that it was calculated from a demonstration building and a sample plot. For a data company credibility is the product, so we did not hide it.

03Search engine optimisation

Three languages, one page per service

Behind the striking homepage, full search engine optimisation was done too. Everything below can be checked on the live site.

Hungarian, English and German
All eight pages in all three languages, each with its own title and description per language, written for the search term used there: quantity takeoff is Mengenermittlung in German and mennyiségszámítás in Hungarian.
A separate page for each service
The 360° camera, the site survey and the quantity takeoff each got their own page with their own title and description — so each can answer its own search.
Languages linked
hreflang annotations on every language version, with a default version, and a canonical URL on every page — Google knows which language to show to whom.
Structured data
The service pages describe themselves to search engines as a service, with a breadcrumb trail.
Sitemap and robots.txt
All 24 pages are in the sitemap, and robots.txt points search engines to it.

04What isn't here yet

There are deliberately no result figures

The site is new and its rankings are being built now. We will put traffic or enquiry figures here once they can be measured and the client has approved them — never an estimate.
