The backstory

This project
is personal.

Stratos Web exists so that ambitious brands look ambitious online. This programme, though, started somewhere else entirely.

Our founder created the Impact Program because addiction touched people close to him. Seeing those struggles up close made it clear how vital organisations working in rehabilitation, prevention and mental health are — and how often an outdated website holds them back while they do extraordinary work.

The goal was never simply to build websites. The goal is for these organisations to reach more of the people who need them, and to have more impact than ever.

— The Stratos Web team

Help

Frequently
asked

Can't find your answer? Write to us — we're glad to help.

Is it really free?

Yes, entirely. Selected organisations pay nothing. There are no hidden fees, licence costs or obligations. We cover the design, the development and the launch.

Why do you do this?

Because we've seen first hand how vital organisations working in rehabilitation, mental health and child protection are. We have the expertise to help, so we help.

Who can apply?

Nonprofit organisations that create real, measurable social impact and are open to working together. Priority goes to organisations working in recovery, mental health and child protection.

How many organisations do you select?

Only a handful each year. We deliberately keep the number small so every organisation gets our full attention and the highest quality website.

Do you maintain the website?

Every organisation gets initial technical support after launch. Longer-term needs we discuss together during the conversation.

Do you also redesign existing websites?

Of course. Whether you're starting from scratch or replacing an outdated site, we build a modern, professional presence from the ground up.
