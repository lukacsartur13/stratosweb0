The backstory

This project
is personal.

Stratos Webdesign exists so that ambitious brands look ambitious online. This programme started somewhere else.

Our founder created the Impact Program because addiction touched people close to him. Seeing those struggles up close made it clear how vital organisations working in rehabilitation, prevention and mental health are, and how often an outdated website holds them back while they do extraordinary work.

The goal is for these organisations to reach more of the people who need them, and to have a greater impact.

05The limits

What the programme does not include

We state this in advance, because disappointment is worse than rejection. If any of these rules your organisation out, it's better to know now.

Selection is not guaranteed Applying does not mean the organisation joins the programme. We can work with only a few organisations at a time, so we have to turn down good applications as well.
Not financial support The programme provides work, not funding. We cannot provide donations, operating support or matching funds for grant applications.
Not unlimited scope This is a website of a defined scope, which we agree together after the application. Anything beyond that has to be agreed separately.
It doesn't replace the organisation's own work The content (text, photographs, figures) needs the organisation's involvement. Without it, even the best website stays empty.
Not immediate Both assessment and delivery take time. If you need the website by a fixed date, say so in your application.

Help

Frequently asked
questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us — we're glad to help.

Is it really free?

Yes. Selected organisations don't pay for the design, the development or the launch; we cover those. For the content (text, photographs), though, we need your involvement.

Why do you do this?

Because we've seen first hand how vital organisations working in rehabilitation, mental health and child protection are. We have the expertise to help, so we help.

Who can apply?

Nonprofit organisations that create real, measurable social impact and are open to working together. Priority goes to organisations working in recovery, mental health and child protection.

How many organisations do you select?

Only a handful each year. We deliberately keep the number small so every organisation gets our full attention and the highest quality website.

Do you maintain the website?

After launch you get technical support for the start-up period. Longer-term needs we discuss together during the conversation.

Do you also redesign existing websites?

Yes. Whether you're starting from scratch or replacing an outdated site, we build a modern, professional presence from the ground up.
