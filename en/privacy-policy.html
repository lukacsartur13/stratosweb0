Purposes of processing

The Controller processes personal data for the following purposes:

  • preparing quotes and client communication

Categories of personal data processed

When contacting us or requesting a quote

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • company name (if provided)
  • any other information given in the message
  • the device identification string your browser sends (user agent)
  • a salted, irreversible fingerprint of the IP address — the IP address itself is not stored

When the project questionnaire is completed

  • the above, and the answers given in the questionnaire

With every submission

  • which page the submission was sent from, and which page was landed on first
  • the domain name of the referring website (without the full referring address)
  • the campaign labels in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term), if there are any
  • how long the form took to fill in, and the screen orientation

The campaign labels come only from the five parameters above: every other value in the link is ignored. We do not read or store advertising click identifiers (such as gclid or fbclid). These values stay in your browser's session storage until you close the tab, and reach us only if you actually submit a form.

When booking a consultation

  • name
  • email address
  • company name and phone number (if provided)
  • the topic of the conversation and the time booked
  • an irreversible fingerprint of the link for managing the booking, which only the person booking holds — the link itself is not stored
  • for a time booked in the client portal: the account's name and email address, and the project

When subscribing to the newsletter

  • email address and the chosen language
  • how you subscribed (the newsletter form or the contact form's checkbox), on which page and when
  • when you confirmed, and if you unsubscribe, when and how
  • a salted, irreversible fingerprint of the IP address — the IP address itself is not stored
  • which newsletters we sent you, and when

Subscribing takes two steps: we send a confirmation e-mail to the address given, and send newsletters only once you confirm with the button in it. An unconfirmed request is deleted automatically 30 days after the last confirmation e-mail. Requests recorded before the newsletter launched (18 October 2026) are not treated as subscriptions: they receive at most one confirmation e-mail. You can unsubscribe with one click at the bottom of every newsletter; an address that cannot be delivered to, or that reports our e-mail as spam, is taken off the list.

Retention periods

  • contact data: no more than 5 years
  • booking data: no more than 5 years
  • newsletter data: until you unsubscribe; after that we keep the address only so that we never send it a newsletter again — on request we delete that too. An unconfirmed signup: at most 30 days.
  • invoicing data: 8 years (under the Hungarian Accounting Act)

Google Search Console is not audience measurement and not a data processor: it reports how our own site appears in search, and does not track visitors. The website uses no Meta Pixel and no other social-media tracking code. The social icons in the footer are ordinary links and load nothing.

Use of cookies

By default the website sets no cookies. Cookies are set only by audience measurement, and only where the visitor has explicitly consented in the bar shown at the bottom of the page. Without consent, measurement does not start, no cookie is placed on the device, and no data whatsoever is transmitted to Google.

The website uses no advertising, marketing or profiling cookies. Google Analytics advertising features (Google Signals, ad personalisation) are switched off.

Which cookies may be on your device

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) — only after consent. The cookies named _ga and _ga_<identifier> distinguish returning visits and sessions. They contain no name, email address or phone number.
  • Remembering your choice — the decision is kept in the browser’s local storage (stratos.consent). This is not a cookie and never reaches the server; it exists solely so that, after a refusal, you are not asked again on every page.

What we measure, and what we do not

Measurement covers which pages are read, how far into them visitors get, and which buttons they click. The data sent for measurement never includes a name, email address, phone number, company name, text typed into a form, questionnaire answers, or the identifier of an enquiry or submission. This is not merely policy: before sending, the measurement code checks the field names and discards, in its entirety, any event containing a prohibited field.

Withdrawing consent

Consent can be withdrawn at any time, exactly as easily as it was given: the Cookie settings button in the footer reopens the choice so it can be changed. On withdrawal, measurement stops immediately and the website deletes any _ga cookies already set. Cookies can also be deleted or blocked at any time in the browser’s own settings.

Refusing consent does not restrict use of the website in any way: the site works in full without measurement.

Definitions

  • Processor: a natural or legal person, public authority, agency or other body which processes personal data on behalf of the controller.
  • Processing: any operation or set of operations performed on personal data or sets of personal data, whether or not by automated means — for example collection, recording, organisation, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, disclosure, restriction, erasure or destruction.
  • Transfer: making personal data processed by the Controller accessible to third parties.
  • Personal data breach: a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, personal data processed.
  • Identifiable natural person: one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, by reference to an identifier.
  • Recipient: a natural or legal person, public authority, agency or other body to which personal data is disclosed.
  • Consent of the data subject: a freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous indication of the data subject's wishes.
  • Data subject: a natural person identified or identifiable on the basis of any information.
  • Users: visitors to the websites operated by the Controller.
  • Third party: anyone other than the data subject, the controller or the processor.
  • Personal data: any information relating to the data subject.
