The comparison

Why is it better
than paying
once?

A bespoke website typically costs several thousand euros up front, and the work doesn't stop there: hosting, updates, fixes, changes. Without them the site is outdated within a few years.

In the monthly model there's no large initial outlay and your site is never left on its own. What breaks, we fix. What ages, we update. Predictable cost, predictable operation.

  • No lock-in and no penalty clause — cancellable monthly.
  • The domain and all content stay yours.
  • Modular build: extendable, even with an online shop.
  • Responsive design, tested on every device type.

Help

Frequently
asked

Can't find your answer? Write to us — we're glad to help.

How long does a website take?

A simpler website is typically ready in 1–2 weeks once we have the content we need (copy, images, brand assets). For more complex sites with more features this can grow to 3–4 weeks, and we keep you informed at every step.

Why is a monthly fee better than a large one-off cost?

You don't have to commit to a four-figure investment in one go; you can start your online presence with a predictable, lower monthly cost. The fee also covers ongoing maintenance, hosting, updates and support, so no unexpected extra costs turn up.

How custom will my website be?

Your website is built around your brand, your visual identity and your goals — we don't work from a template with no creative room. The design, the copy and the features all follow the needs of your business.

Can the system be extended later?

Yes. The website is modular, so new pages, features or even an online shop can be added as your business grows. You don't have to decide everything at the start — the system grows with you.

Will the site work properly on phones and tablets?

Yes. Every website is responsive, so it adapts automatically to phones, tablets and desktops. We test this on every device type before launch.

Do you also manage Google and Meta ads?

Yes. On request we can help set up and run your Google and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertising, from targeting to continuous campaign optimisation. That way the traffic reaching your site is in expert hands too.

Will you help me understand how it all works?

Of course — we do not leave you on your own after handover. We explain how your site works in plain terms and go through the concepts that matter, so you can make decisions with confidence.

How is the performance of the site and the ads tracked?

We provide clear statistics for both the website and the ads, showing traffic, the key metrics and campaign results. On request we also summarise performance in regular, plain-language reports.

Who owns the website and the domain?

The domain and all content are yours. We operate the site while the subscription runs; if you cancel, we of course hand over the domain and your content.

Is there a minimum term or a penalty?

No. The contract can be cancelled monthly, with no hidden costs. You stay as long as you're happy with the service.

What do I need to provide to get started?

A few basics about your business, plus copy and images if you have them. If you don't, we help with the wording and with choosing images, so that's never what holds the launch up.

What if I already have a website?

We take over existing sites too. We review what's worth keeping and either refresh it or build a new one in its place; your content and your domain stay with you.
