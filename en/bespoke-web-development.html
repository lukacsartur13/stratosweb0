Sound familiar?

A buyer searches for your products. They find a PDF catalogue, a contact form and an old photo of the factory hall. What you can do, in what sizes and in what materials, they have to find out by phone. In the end they make that call to the competitor whose site showed them what they were looking for first time.

Meanwhile your salespeople answer the same questions for every new prospect, and the serious quote requests get lost among the people who are just browsing.

A site that answers in advance

We build corporate websites where the product, the specifications and the references are clearly visible in one place, and quote requests reach you already carrying the details needed for a decision.

The process

How we get to handover

Five steps. At the end of each one you know where we are and what comes next.

01

Free discovery call

We talk through what you make, who your customers are and what the new site has to do.

02

Tailored proposal

You get an itemised proposal: what will be built, what will not, and by when.

03

Starting out

Once the deposit arrives, we schedule the project and the work begins.

04

Design

We show you the design, and you shape it. We present it so that management, sales and the technical side can all give their feedback. Three rounds of revisions come with it.

05

Build and handover

We build it, test it on every device, put it live and hand over the access and the files. If you want, we go on running it for you afterwards.

The comparison

Why
one team?

If one agency builds the website, another handles SEO and a third runs the ads, then when something goes wrong everyone points at someone else. With us, the website, SEO, advertising and operation are in one pair of hands, with one contact person.

  • The source code, the files and all the content are yours, with no platform lock-in.
  • Bespoke code: fast loading, and only what you need goes in.
  • The website is a one-off, itemised project fee; operation and SEO are available as a monthly partnership.
  • We take over existing sites too: we review what is worth keeping and make a proposal.

Help

Frequently asked
questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us and let's talk it through.

Who owns the source code and the content?

You do. At handover you get the access and the files, and you are not tied to any one supplier.

Can it be built in several languages?

Yes. We build and optimise for search in Hungarian, English and German, with its own title and description for each language.

Will our website have 3D?

If it helps to show the product, yes. If not, we won't force it.

What availability and response time do you commit to?

If you also leave running the website to us, we typically commit to 99.9% availability and a response within 4 hours to a critical fault. The exact levels are set in the contract.

Do you take over existing sites?

Yes. First we review what is worth keeping and which pages of the old site bring in traffic, so that it isn't lost on the new one.

What does it cost?

Every proposal is individual. After the free discovery call you get an itemised proposal.

How long does it take?

We set the deadline in the proposal and guarantee it, provided all the materials arrive on time.

How do you work if we have several decision-makers?

We coordinate with one contact person and hand over the designs in a way that lets everyone else give feedback too. The three rounds of revisions are best used for your combined feedback.
