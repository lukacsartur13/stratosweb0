01How they connect

The website is the centre. The rest brings it traffic and credibility.

Most projects start with the website, because it is the one channel you own outright. The brand identity decides whether the site looks credible; advertising and SEO decide whether anyone reaches it at all.

That's what tells you which service you need. If you don't have a site yet, running ads is throwing money away. If you have a site but nobody finds it, the site isn't the bottleneck.

03Combinations

What actually works together?

We're not listing this so you buy more. We're listing it because the wrong order is the most common reason a project produces nothing.

Identity, then website
If you don't have a settled look, the same decisions have to be made while designing the site anyway. In the wrong order you pay for them twice.
Website, then advertising
Advertising buys traffic. If that traffic lands on a site that doesn’t convince, the spend only magnifies the site’s faults.
Website and SEO together
Most of SEO is structure, speed and copy. While the site is being built these are part of the work; afterwards they mean rebuilding.
Website, then monthly SEO
The work doesn’t stop at handover. The same team that built the site carries it forward, so nobody has to get to know it all over again.
Advertising only
Worth it on its own when you already have a site that measurably works and traffic really is the bottleneck.
Identity only
It happens: the site works, but the brand hasn't kept up with the business. Then the identity is a complete project on its own.

04The proposal

How is the proposal put together?

We don’t publish a price list because an accurate one would have so many lines that nobody could find their way through it. Instead, this is how the proposal comes together.

Free discovery call

We find out what your business does, who your customer is, what exists today, and what you want to achieve.

Scope

This is where it becomes clear: how many pages, which features, what content, who writes the copy, who supplies the images.

Written proposal

For that scope you get a price and a schedule, valid for 14 days. If something is left out, that is in it too, so you don’t find out halfway through.

Go or no go

The proposal is not an obligation. If you say yes, the work starts once the deposit arrives.

The website is a one-off project fee, and it is yours. You can add SEO and maintenance alongside it as a monthly partnership.
