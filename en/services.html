01How they connect

The website is the centre. The rest adds traffic and meaning.

Most projects start with the website, because it's the only surface you fully own. The brand identity decides whether it looks credible; the advertising decides whether anyone gets there at all.

That's what tells you which service you need. If you don't have a site yet, running ads is throwing money away. If you have a site but nobody finds it, the site isn't the bottleneck.

03Combinations

What actually works together?

We're not listing this so you buy more. We're listing it because the wrong order is the most common reason a project produces nothing.

Identity, then website
If you don't have a settled look, the same decisions have to be made while designing the site anyway. In the wrong order you pay for them twice.
Website, then advertising
Advertising buys traffic. If that traffic lands on an unconvincing page, the spending scales up the page's faults.
Website and SEO together
Most of search optimisation is structure, speed and text — free while the site is being built, a rebuild afterwards.
Advertising only
Worth it on its own when you already have a site that measurably works and traffic really is the bottleneck.
Identity only
It happens: the site works, but the brand hasn't kept up with the business. Then the identity is a complete project on its own.

04The proposal

How is the quote put together?

There's no price list on this site because an honest one would need so many lines that it would be useless. This is what there is instead.

Understanding the requirement

In writing or in conversation. It establishes what your business does, who your customer is, what exists now, and what you want to achieve.

Scope

That becomes the scope: how many pages, which features, what content, which integrations, who writes the copy, who supplies the images.

Written proposal

You get a price and a schedule for that scope. Anything left out is named as well — so it doesn't surface halfway through.

Go or no go

The proposal isn't a commitment. If the project isn't worth it to you on these terms, it's better to know at the start.
