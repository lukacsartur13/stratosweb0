Two channels, one system.

Paid and organic search together became a measurable client acquisition channel for Rapidkert: organic search brought in approximately 110 million HUF in quote requests, advertising around 9 million HUF.

The two figures are kept apart because they arose apart. The 110 million HUF is the total value of quote requests that came in from organic search, with no ad spend behind it. The 9 million HUF was brought in by advertising. So the ratio applies to the 9 million HUF from Google Ads alone.

04The digital reinvention

Performance built the pipeline. Design built the position.

Once client acquisition was working, the question changed. From then on the aim was no longer for Rapidkert to keep pace with other landscaping websites, but to rethink what a landscaping website could feel like.

05Below the surface

A great garden starts below the surface.

Most landscaping websites show the same things: finished lawns, plants, terraces, completed gardens. All of it the part you can see.

But the quality of a garden is not decided at the surface. Soil preparation, drainage, irrigation, underground systems, structural preparation: these decide what the garden looks like in two years. The customer sees none of it.

This became the visual and conceptual foundation of the website: we turned Rapidkert's invisible expertise into the central visual idea. Instead of another gallery, we built an experience that shows what keeps those gardens beautiful.

06Interactive 3D experience

The surface is only the final layer

At the centre of the opening screen there is no photograph but a three-dimensional cross-section of a garden. The 3D here is how the argument is made, not decoration.

Three-dimensional cross-section
The subject of the opening screen is a cut block of ground: the finished garden surface on top, with lawn and paving slabs, and the layers below it.
Underground layers
Topsoil, compacted subsoil and gravel bed one below the other, visibly separated: the structure the finished garden hides.
Irrigation and drainage
The irrigation line and the drainage pipe run through the section at the depth they sit at in reality.
Depth readout
A depth readout in metres sits along the right edge, reading zero at the surface: the site talks about depth in its own unit.
Scrolling down, not forward
The site opens with the instruction "scroll below the surface": the direction of the scroll is the story itself.
Typography and palette
A large, dense grotesque for the headline, monospace technical labels, and a restrained natural palette: deep green, earth and sand tones, warm cream.
Bilingual interface
Hungarian and English versions, switchable from the header.

Everything described above can be checked on the published rapidkert.com opening screen.
