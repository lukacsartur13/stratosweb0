Stratos / Work / Rapidkert Kft.

Rapidkert Kft. — landscaping and irrigation

A garden you want to look at after dark, too.

Landscaping is a high-value investment with a long decision time. The site doesn't try to shorten that decision — it shows the result and offers a conversation.

Sector landscaping, irrigation What we delivered website Language Hungarian Live address rapidkert.com

01Client context

Nobody commissions a garden on impulse.

Landscaping and irrigation installation are services where a visitor cannot compare prices from a list, because no two gardens are the same. So the site doesn't have to communicate price — it has to communicate competence and result.

That determined the structure: the opening screen doesn't show a list of services, it shows a finished garden — at night, lit, in the state the client is paying for.

02Scope delivered

What the finished site contains

Everything below can be checked on the published site.

Opening screen
A full-width photograph with the service named on it: "Automatic irrigation system installation and landscaping". No slogan standing in for the service.
A single primary request
There is one button on the first screen and it leads to a free consultation. Not a quote, not a download, not a newsletter.
Brand mark
A dark header carrying the company logo and strapline, which stays readable over the bright photograph.
Direct contact
A permanently available contact element at the bottom of the screen, so a question can be asked at any point in the scroll.
Responsive layout
A layout that works on phone, tablet and desktop alike.

03Design direction

The photograph carries it; the typography doesn't compete

A classical serif in the headline, light type over the dark parts of the photograph. The typography is deliberately restrained: the photograph is the proof, the text only names what the visitor is looking at.

The lit evening garden wasn't an aesthetic decision. A daytime garden photograph shows a garden; an evening one, shot with the lighting on, shows that the garden was designed — and that is what a contractor gets hired for.

The photography came from the client. Stratos designed how it is used and laid out, not the photography itself.

04What isn't here yet

There are deliberately no result figures

There is no visitor count, enquiry count, conversion rate or revenue figure on this page, because none of them is available to us in verifiable form. Until the client supplies measurement data, this section stays empty rather than estimated.

REQUIRES USER FACTUAL APPROVAL — project duration, the exact delivery date, the technology used, and any measured result or client quote.
