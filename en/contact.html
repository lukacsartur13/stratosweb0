01Who we work with

It works best when the website has a job to do.

We work with sole traders, small and medium businesses, and the marketing teams of larger companies. What they have in common isn't size — it's that the website or the advertising has to achieve something: an enquiry, a booking, an order, an applicant.

If you don't know what a realistic goal is, that isn't a problem. The consultation is more about that than about technology.

  • A new website, or a rebuild of an existing one.
  • Brand identity, copywriting and search optimisation alongside the website.
  • Managing Google and Meta advertising.
  • Non-profits: the Impact Program runs on a separate route.

03What follows

What happens after you send it?

No automated sales sequence and no cold calling. Four steps, and you've already taken the first.

Confirmation

The form confirms straight away that it arrived. We usually reply by email to the address you gave us within one working day.

Free consultation

An online conversation, usually 30–45 minutes. We go through what your site does now, what you want to achieve, and what that actually requires.

Tailored proposal

After the conversation we send a written proposal: what we build, on what schedule, and what it costs. The price follows the complexity of the work, not a package decided in advance.

Decision

The proposal carries no obligation. If this isn't the right moment, we'll say so — that's cheaper for both of us than a bad project.
