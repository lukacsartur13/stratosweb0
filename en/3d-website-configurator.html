When explaining eats up your sales time

Today your salesperson is explaining the same thing for the third time: what sizes it comes in, what it is made of, what the difference is between the two versions. They send the PDF, draw a sketch, arrange a demo.

Meanwhile the customer has asked two other companies for a quote, and sees the same thing on all three websites: a photo on a white background and a contact form.

After a while you take it for granted that a quote takes weeks to come together. Meanwhile your product is put in the same category as your cheapest competitor's, because the difference doesn't show on the website.

Show it the way you would see it in the workshop.

On a 3D website the customer explores your product for themselves: they rotate it, zoom in, switch layers on and off. In a configurator they also put together their own version. By the time they request a quote, they already know what they want.

What is a 3D website, and what is a product configurator?

3D website

A 3D website is a website on which visitors can look at a product or service in three dimensions, interactively: rotate it, take it apart layer by layer, or move through it by scrolling. It pays off where customers find it hard to picture what they will get, such as the structure of a garden below ground or the survey of a building.

Product configurator

A product configurator is a tool built into a website in which customers put the product together themselves: they choose the size, material, colour or accessories and see the result straight away. Fewer questions, faster decisions, and the premium version is easier to sell too, because the customer can see what they get.

Three reasons 3D pays for itself

01

Customers see what they are buying

They don't have to imagine the size, the material or the difference between versions, because they can see it. They get in touch with fewer questions, decide faster, and your sales team's time goes to the serious prospects.

02

You stand out in the market

Your competitors' sites have a photo and a PDF. On yours, the customer walks all the way around the product. That is what they remember when they compare the three quotes.

03

The premium price makes sense

When customers can see the details, the quality and the finish, it is easier for them to understand why it costs more. The 3D experience makes the more expensive product sellable too.

What people usually ask about 3D

It's a serious investment.

It is. With a high-value product, one or two orders won usually pay for it. The proposal shows you item by item where the money goes, and on the discovery call we will also tell you if a good website without 3D would be enough for you.

Will Google see it?

Yes. Alongside the 3D, the content is there as text too, so search engines understand the page as well.

We don't have time for it.

We do the work. From you we need the product data, the models or drawings, and one contact person who signs off at the key points.

Who is it for?

It's for you if

  • your product is hard to show in a photo, because what matters is inside it, in its layers or in its versions;
  • much of your salespeople's time goes on explaining;
  • you cost more than your competitors and want that to make sense at first glance;
  • your product comes in many variations (size, material, colour, accessories);
  • you sell to B2B customers, where a single order is worth a lot.

It's not for you if

  • a quick, simple company site is enough: then our web design for small business page is the right place to start;
  • the 3D would only be decoration and would not help the decision.
