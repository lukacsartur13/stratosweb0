03What they share

What they share isn't a style, it's the method.

The three sites deliberately don't resemble each other. A barbershop needs bookings, a landscaper needs to build trust ahead of a high-value investment, and a mental coach needs to establish a personal connection. The same template would be wrong for all three.

  • Every site is designed from scratch — not a theme recoloured.
  • The opening screen has one job, and it's measurable.
  • The content speaks the business's language, not marketing language.
  • Responsive layout, tested on both phone and desktop.
